# The Penalty Box

A collector shows off trading cards that captured the most notorious hockey fights of a half-forgotten league. Every card is a little piece of rink folklore: a frozen moment, a blurry photograph, and the story people still tell about it. Invent the teams, players, arenas, and legendary scraps for each episode.

## The cards

Most cards catch two opposing players squared up on the ice, jerseys pulled crooked and teammates rushing into the frame. A few show the stranger moments everyone remembers: spectators pouring onto the rink, a player taking out the referee by accident, or a toothless grin for the camera after the final whistle. The photographs should feel like sports pictures from their era, complete with harsh arena light, motion blur, and scuffed card stock. Give each card a short caption and a back that tells the story behind the shot.

## The box

The collection is kept in a narrow open-top card box, the kind made for trading cards, with the cards filed on edge. It has no lid and is never unpacked on camera: it already stands open at the side of the table when the episode begins, and the collector simply reaches in and pulls out a card.

## The host

The host loves the absurd little details as much as the fights: a loose skate, a mascot fleeing the boards, the same furious fan appearing in three different photographs. They talk like a collector showing a friend their favorite finds, with quick jokes and the occasional story worth slowing down for.

## An episode

Pull a handful of cards and follow the rivalries, accidents, and unlikely heroes they uncover. Let the pictures surprise the host. End with the card that best captures why this chaotic league is still remembered.
