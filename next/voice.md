# Voice: speech for ghost, from robotic sources

**Goal.** Ghost videos with spoken narration and subtitles over its 3D worlds, under two hard constraints: no generative AI in this synth (nothing pretrained, nothing trained on other people's voices, everything procedural and inspectable) and no vocal recording sessions. The human writes the script; the machine finds the voice. The bet: a realistic-enough voice can be built from robotic, old-school audio sources plus careful modulation, sparse human labels, tiny in-engine ML, and EMA convergence - the machinery ghost already runs on. (The neural readers, Piper and Kokoro, are in [voice_engines.md](voice_engines.md); this note is the formant synth.)

**Status.** The formant synth is one of the Voice card's voices (`formant`, [`FormantVoice`](../src/voice/formant_voice.gd)): `VoiceHost` makes its sentences in-process and answers as the host answers for a neural voice, so a reading, Test and an export read in it, at the host's 22,050 Hz. A tab keeps its voice on its row (`formant:` - the lineage, the traits, the frozen genome, the reception it was caught at, the sustain bank). The Lab on a formant tab is the fishing game ([`VoiceFishing`](../src/reading/voice_fishing.gd)): the line reads the Test passage on a player of its own, every voice cast or held is the tab's, and the Collection, mode and reserve are the player's (`[synth]` in ghost.cfg). Of a tab's rows it takes the Tone's pace and pitch, the Pace, the Pause and the room; Dynamics, Effort and Arc are the neural host's, hidden for it. Listen records through the system's default microphone, reading the Test passage. Gates: formant_voice_check, fishing_bank_check, reader_browser_check. Open: the author's ear on a reading in a bred voice beside the neural ones.

**What it is.** Source-filter synthesis (the Klatt / eSpeak lineage) with a sampled population of renditions, content-addressed sparse supervision, and a Fujisaki-style prosody realization. Every part has a 1980s-DSP name; none of it is a black box.

## The three-layer decomposition (this is what makes it possible)

Speech factors cleanly, and each factor has a different owner:

1. **Source** - the excitation: a periodic buzz plus noise. Any pitched, harmonically rich sample (a cello note, a synth pad, a buzzer) can serve as the glottal source once it is pitch-tracked and looped. The source carries timbre and identity, and is a sampled axis ("cattle, not pets" over voices).
2. **Filter** - formant resonances (a handful of bandpass filters whose center frequencies move through time). Intelligibility lives only here. A vowel is a formant configuration; a consonant is a transition plus noise shaping. The samples cannot carry this, so the filter layer is rule-driven from the script: per-phoneme formant targets from a small built-in table, interpolated through time - what a talkbox or vocoder does.
3. **Prosody** - f0 contour, per-phoneme durations, pauses, energy envelope: cadence and inflection, the difference between robotic and alive. It is low-dimensional (a few scalars per syllable), which is why tiny models are enough.

Layers 1 and 2 alone are the robotic-but-intelligible baseline. Layer 3 is where the work is.

## The pipeline

1. **The human writes the script.** Alignment is solved by construction: the audio is synthesized, so every phoneme's position is known exactly - no ASR, no forced alignment. Pronunciation can be co-authored (a phonetic spelling convention, a small exceptions dictionary), so no G2P dependency is needed to start.
2. **Population render.** The script goes through the synth many times (500), every tunable sampled from ranges: source sample, source pitch and loop character, formant scale (vocal tract length), f0 base and range, speaking rate, pause distribution, jitter and shimmer. The result is a grounded distribution of candidate voices, seeded and reproducible per (script, seed).
3. **Sparse landmark labels.** The human listens to the opening of a rendition and plants a few landmarks - typed pressure points ("pitch resets here", "lean on this syllable", "let this pause breathe"). Mechanically these are Dial deposits: a typed channel (pitch / duration / energy / pause), a signed amount, a decay.
4. **Generalization by content, not position.** A landmark is keyed to the harmonic and sequencing context where it was planted (`HarmonicSignature`, the Echo discipline: corrections belong to what the audio is, never to where the playhead was). A small nonlinearity learns the activation of each pressure from context features (phrase position, stress pattern, phoneme class, local signature), so when the script's structures recur the pressures re-fire there, unlabeled.
5. **EMA realization.** Targets jump discretely (per syllable); the realized contour converges smoothly. This is the accepted model of human f0 (Fujisaki, 1984: phrase and accent commands smoothed through decaying second-order filters; declination is the slow EMA, accents are fast ones riding it). The same holds for the energy envelope and for formant transitions (coarticulation is target undershoot under smoothing).

## Why the existing machinery carries almost all of this

- **Storyboard / Cast / Track**: a voice is an actor; an utterance is a track span with `on:` cues; subtitles fall out because the text spans and their timing are the same data.
- **Dial**: the deposit mechanics (surge, decay, standing pattern, additive, seeded) are the labeling primitive.
- **Echo / HarmonicSignature**: content-addressing for label generalization.
- **Nonlinear**: the response-curve library is the activation shape of the pressure model.
- **Bake / export**: utterances render offline; the exported voice is byte-stable per (script, seed, labels).
- **Tiny ML in Godot**: inference is per syllable (control rate), a few hundred to a few thousand parameters, evaluable in GDScript in microseconds. The audio-rate work is DSP, done once per utterance into a cached WAV. Training on a handful of labels is small enough for in-engine SGD.

## Honestly graded

- **The cliff is intelligibility, not naturalness.** If the filter layer cannot make a sound-font source say a recognizable word, prosody polish is irrelevant, so the rungs hit the cliff first: one vowel, one word, one sentence. The fallback is a plain pulse-train source (pure Klatt); the population, label and EMA machinery are untouched.
- **Sparse labels generalizing is a bet.** It is plausible for prosody because prosodic structure recurs, and unproven for segment-level fixes. If landmarks from the opening do not transfer, the scheme degrades to a performance instrument: hand-planted deposits along the whole timeline.
- **The ceiling is stylized, not photoreal.** Modulated robotic renditions with good prosody should not try to fool anyone into hearing a recording. The target is alive, intentional, expressive, and unmistakably synthetic. "Natural" means natural cadence, not a cloned human.
- **No per-experiment tuning.** Constants are fixed and model-agnostic; the only signals are endogenous (script structure, harmonic context) and the human's labels. The population size is not a hyperparameter to sweep.
- **Dependency posture.** Nothing new to start: sources from samples we have rights to, filters and pitch tracking in-house (the FFT and bake machinery exists), the phoneme table in-house. eSpeak-NG is a reference to read, not a dependency.

## Rungs (each falsifiable before the next)

- [x] **Rung 0 - the vowel.** `Voice` synthesizes vowels through a three-formant cascade from a pulse-train source (sampled sound-font sources are the open half; the source is behind one function). `tests/voice/voice_check.gd` verifies them with Goertzel probes: /AA/ dominates its F1 region by about 200x and /IY/ its F2 region by about 4x. Judged by ear: pending.
- [x] **Rung 1 - the word.** `Phonemes` (table, digraph and magic-e rules, exceptions, `[K AE T]` inline phonetics); full sentences render with monotonic word and phoneme timing maps, stops, fricatives and aspiration. Robotic on purpose.
- [~] **Rung 2 - the population.** The speaker is a trait vector (`Voice.TRAIT_KEYS`: pitch / lilt / tract / pace / breath / grit / drawl, each in [-1,1]). The zero vector is the hand-curated default speaker (its centres live in `Spec.from_traits`), a seed only initializes the sliders, and the vector is the replicable identity (autosaved; asserted byte-identical in `voice_check`). The N-render batch and side-by-side audition are open.
- [x] **Real-time streaming.** Synthesis runs about 30x real time in GDScript, so the Lab's line (`VoiceStream`) runs a sliding window: a ~0.3 s prebuffer is synthesized in the triggering frame, pushed into an `AudioStreamGenerator` on a player of its own, and a worker thread keeps a lead ahead of the playhead, decoupled from the render loop. Finished takes loop on the thread. A reading is made a sentence at a time as any voice's is (`FormantVoice.say`). Output level is a fixed calibrated gain (`Voice.OUT_GAIN`; raw cascade peak ~3.3 across trait extremes), not retroactive normalization, so live, WAV and loop match.
- [x] **Implicit speaking (no Speak button).** A cast speaks at once. Timbre traits (pitch / tract / breath / grit) retune the running line (`VoiceStream.retune`, an atomic spec swap the worker reads per chunk); structural changes (a new voice, a Hold) restart it in place.
- [x] **Rung 2.5 - the walk and the belt.** The reading is stateful: `ProsodyWalk`, a homeostatic word-by-word walk over sliding EMAs. Arousal opens hot and settles (hot is fast, settled is slow). Spent emphasis is a windowed EMA that spaces emphases out (emphasized words get a pre-pause, stretch, rise). Breath debt forces real pauses in unpunctuated text. A seeded motif vocabulary (tilt / lean / gap gestures) recurs per sentence. The genome is sampled through a lineage: a linear chain of seeds where the root samples every parameter and each later seed perturbs by 0.6^generation, so Evolve refines a kept reading instead of re-rolling it. The Collection (up to 23 seeds) captures {lineage, traits, genome, reception}: a kept seed reproduces its reading exactly. Breath emergence and lineage byte-determinism are asserted in `voice_check`.
- [x] **Rung 2.75 - the inventory and the attention reward.** The belt is an active population with an implicit reward: hold time. While a reading plays, every seed shaping it accrues listened seconds. Each belt entry tracks plays, hold seconds, kept (restores), evolved (children spawned) and caught (descendants captured); the acceptance metric is hold-per-play relative to the belt's average, shown per seed in the inventory rows. Synthesis blends 1+N genomes - a fixed population prior (the range midpoints, the regularizer), the working lineage, and every toggled seed, uniform mean, motif vocabularies pooled. Blend determinism is asserted in `voice_check`.
- [x] **Rung 2.9 - sparse activations over the prose** (motifs v2). Ghost's `Activation` discipline applied to words: four independent channels (stretch / pitch / echo / swell), each a thresholded nonlinearity over seeded drive with a fast-attack slow-decay refractory, so events are sparse and self-spacing (about 0-1 echo strikes per 44 words, lineage-dependent). Firing feeds a damped resonance ring that colors the following seconds and invites neighboring firings. Pitch attractors: a pooled per-voice anchor shelf the melody gravitates toward (genome `gravity`), with pitch activations jumping most of the way. Echo is a real feedback delay line in the synthesis output; only activated words are sent into it, and it rings through the pauses. All genome-carried.
- [x] **Rung 2.95 - the throw loop.** One gesture: Throw, it grows or it doesn't, Keep or don't. A throw parents itself from the belt weighted by acceptance (sometimes wild), inheriting lineage and traits with generation-decaying jitter, and speaks immediately. No toggles or blend controls: ancestors accrue hold time from their descendants' listening. A kept seed's tooltip reads out the genome's implications (temperament, breath span, pitch gravity, ring, strike bar, pace range) plus its behavioral ledger. Cross-seed influence statistics are deferred.
- [x] **Rung 2.99 - reward profiles + catch mechanics.** Reward is profiled per session: Snap (default; decision latency earns, 2.4*8/(8+t)), Drift (sitting with it earns), Hunt (distance earns, and the throws range wilder). Keep is a catch attempt: difficulty is the nearest-neighbor distance from the candidate to the bank in trait and genome space; foreign seeds fight (p = 1 - 0.75*d) and can break free, with retry allowed (in Snap the clock keeps running). A caught seed records its reward and difficulty; ancestors share the reward. Acceptance reads 20*reward + hold/30 per play. The catch is animated by a code-drawn orb in the seed's hue; the roll is decided before the orb appears.
- [x] **Rung 2.995 - disfluency and wobble.** `sentence_end()` draws each ending fresh (lengthening 0.75-1.45x, falls of varied depth, 12% flat, questions rising by varied amounts), since fixed terminal constants cloned every sentence closing. Hesitation is an activation channel: sparse gaps or filled "um"s land before words (genome `hesit_bias` makes some voices fluent, some halting); the authored `%HESITATION` token always renders one, shown as an ellipsis in the karaoke. Swing: every activation kicks a cadence offset (rush or drag by seeded coin) that folds back into the running pace and decays. Open: word repeats and false starts.
- [x] **Rung 2.999 - bites and cards.** The planner knows when every strike will play, so the stream publishes its event times; a strike opens a detection window that decays (tau 2 s), and catch odds are dominated by bite freshness. The effect is genome-carried regardless. A successful catch presents the seed as a constellation: lines seeded from the lineage, color attuned to cosine similarity against the party's centre (kin warm, foreign cold; 18-dim trait and genome space). Accept folds it in and every member's color re-attunes; Release changes nothing.
- [x] **Rung 2.9999 - easy fishing and the adrenochrome.** Bites are latent: a strike latches an anchor that pulls for minutes (45 s decay, occasionally leaving on its own). The two axes are Throw and Pull: pull while it holds and the hook sets or is lost. A set hook begins the reel - up to minutes, scaled by integration difficulty and profile - during which the adrenochrome anneals: the candidate's genome steps through the party's force field (high-acceptance members attract, low-acceptance repel, the prior gently pulls) under cooling noise, then freezes. The frozen creature carries an explicit genome (`Spec.adrenochrome` overrides the walk; identity - motifs, anchors, gates - stays lineage-derived), joins the belt on Accept, and the working voice becomes it. Children inherit frozen genomes verbatim. The HUD is an LCD water panel between the text and the buttons, with the reward profiles as a three-toggle switchboard on its edge.
- [ ] **Rung 3.0 - the room (physical properties).** Candidates, roughly in order of reach: room acoustics (a seeded impulse-response-ish reverb, a large hall versus a quiet booth as a genome axis; the echo bus generalized from one delay tap to a small tap cloud); the crowd (background voices speaking different chunks of the same text, much quieter, lowpassed, unsynchronized - a second and third `VoiceStream` mixed low); air between voice and listener (distance as lowpass plus reverb ratio). All genome-carried, all catchable. The voice should sound like it comes from somewhere.
- [ ] **Rung 3 - landmarks.** Deposit-style labels in the Workspace on the opening; the nonlinear activation model; content-keyed re-firing over the full sequence; A/B against the unlabeled rendition. This is where the sparse-generalization bet is tested. The belt is the coarse version (whole-reading labels), landmarks the fine one (within-reading labels); the acceptance metric is the training signal both grains will share.
- [ ] **Rung 4 - the actor.** Voice as a Cast kind, utterances as Track spans, subtitles rendered from the same spans, export through the bake. A first slice: a formant voice is one of a reading's cast, its takes, karaoke subtitles and export the reading's, every word timed by the synthesizer itself.

## Echo a living voice (2026-07-27, shipped v1)

The Listen button (synth editor loop row) opens a card-style modal: an input device dropdown, a fixed prosody-rich passage to read (statements, a question, an unpunctuated run), Record and Stop. Recording is `AudioStreamMicrophone` -> a muted VoiceIn bus -> `AudioEffectRecord` (the stock Godot pattern). Master is pulled to -60 dB while the mic is open so the looping take cannot bleed into the measurement; project.godot enables driver input.

The measurement (`voice_sampler.gd` `analyze()`, static, on `WorkerThreadPool`): a decimated autocorrelation f0 track with shortest-lag octave protection, energy envelope, boxcar-smoothed syllable-nucleus counting (an envelope-autocorrelation tempo locked onto stress feet), pauses, periodicity and band ratios. These become a trait vector by the closed-form inversions of `Spec.from_traits` (tract is coarse in v1: register and brightness coupling), plus only the measured genome keys (heat, breath_span, spend_window, lean, act_thr, pace_hot/calm) riding the adrenochrome contract; `ProsodyWalk`'s prior backfills the rest. Nothing of the audio survives but about 20 scalars.

The seed is a fresh root lineage, untempered (the trust region caged rolled strangers; this voice is earned by being read). Difficulty is the trait RMS distance from the party centre, and the belt entry is shaped exactly like a catch.

Validation: `tests/voice/sampler_check.gd` round-trips the analysis against synthesized voices with known traits - pitch recovers within tolerance, pace recovers direction, the genome lands in `G_BOUNDS`, it is deterministic, and the minted spec renders. Known coarseness: trait centers are calibrated for human speech (synth voices read slightly biased); tract is a proxy; anchors come from the lineage seed, not the recording.

## Echo v2: formants, melodic modes, measured cadence

- **LPC formant tracking**: Levinson-Durbin (order 10) on pre-emphasized 30 ms vowel-core frames, envelope peaks on a 25 Hz grid, median F1/F2, closed-form tract inversion against F1_REF/F2_REF 458/1720 (the geometric midpoint of tract +-0.7 renders measured by the same estimator). Round trip: +-0.7 truth recovers +-0.68.
- **Melodic modes to anchors**: a smoothed 0.5 st histogram of the voiced semitone track; up to 5 peaks ride the genome's reserved `anchors` key (every generic genome consumer iterates the prior's keys) and replace the walk's seeded shelf in `ProsodyWalk._init`. Gravity is measured too: the fraction of melody sitting on the modes.
- **Cadence as a range**: pace_hot and pace_calm from 2 s windowed syllable-rate p80 and p20 - the recording's own fast and slow. The single "speed" the tooltip shows is one end of the walk's journey.
- **Ornament clamp**: echoed genomes ship damp 0.8, verve 0.15, act_gain 0.7, so the rolled modulator and activation layer is starved and the root is deterministic in the measurement.
- **Shutdown**: the sampler's `_exit_tree` stops the record effect and mic stream. A glibc "double free" abort at exit may be engine-level (audio input driver teardown with `enable_input` on PipeWire); everything is persisted before exit.

## The Resonance: ambience from the voice itself

Ambient sustained tones, implemented as the voice resonating in its own space - no separate music system. A bank of up to 6 tones is tuned to the reading's anchor notes (the walk's pitch attractors; each seed carries its own chord, and a recorded seed's measured melodic modes become its drone). Each tone is an active sine (+0.35 octave for warmth) whose envelope glows by melodic proximity: the note the melody is nearest swells in (~0.3 s), left notes release over ~4 s - long holds through pauses. Deterministic and baked into the take, so exports carry it and the scenes hear it.

Constants (`voice.gd`): `DRONE` (static; tests silence it), `DRONE_STRINGS` 6, `DRONE_NEAR` 2.5 st glow range, `DRONE_ATTACK` 0.3 / `DRONE_RELEASE` 4.0, `DRONE_LEVEL` 0.0025 (drone-only measured at -18.0 dB against the take; the leveler pushes back on attenuation, so it was tuned by A/B, not arithmetic).

Not passive sympathetic-string resonators: at 1.6 Hz bandwidth they never accumulated energy from a jittering f0 (-163 dB). Build the bank with explicit array assignment, never `(st.x as PackedFloat32Array).append()` (the packed-array copy-on-write trap shipped it empty).
